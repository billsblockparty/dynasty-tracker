Glossary
Tap a team for weekly results and the remaining schedule.
Lean / Full Lean keeps each team to its seed, record, streak, standings range, games back and the PF and PA lines; Full adds the rest of the stat lines and the season chart.
Season chart one column per regular-season week. The bar is the team's score (green win, red loss) and the white tick is the opponent's score. When the opponent had the week's highest score its tick is gold with a white frame, and for the week's lowest burgundy with a white frame, matching the #1 and #10 tiles. RANK is where the team's score finished that week out of 10 (#1 gold, 2–3 green, 8–9 red, #10 red with an outline). OPP is where the opponent's score finished, in the same colours (so green there means your opponent scored well). Dashed columns are weeks still to play. W / L chip is the current streak.
PF and PA bars 10 segments, one per league rank: #1 fills all 10, #10 fills 1. Green ranks 1–3, red 8–10, grey otherwise (for PA, #1 is the most points against). Luck bar runs from a centre line: right is lucky, left is unlucky.
Games back how many games behind each line: 1st place, Bye (2nd, the last bye spot) and Play-Off (6th, the last playoff spot). Only the lines a team is behind are shown.
Hi / Lo / Avg highest, lowest and average standings position after each week so far.
Rank chips league rank for each stat. Ranks 1–3 green, 8–10 red, T = tied. PF and PA #1 = most points. Opp rk #1 = hardest schedule. All-play #1 = most all-play wins. Luck #1 = luckiest.
Opp rk average rank of your opponent's score within that week's 10 scores. 1 means you faced the week's top score; 10 means the lowest.
All-play record if you played all 9 teams every week. It measures how well you scored, with your schedule taken out.
Luck actual wins minus expected wins (shown as xW). Expected wins = all-play win % × games played. Example: 44-10 all-play is 81.5%, × 6 games = 4.89 expected wins; with 6 actual wins, luck is +1.11.
Dropped pts per wk the points dropped each week, split by position. Each time a bench player should have started, the gap between him and the starter he replaces (same position, or RB/WR/TE through the flex) is credited to the bench player's position. The six add up to the total.
Tot Eff total (whole-lineup) efficiency: actual points as a share of the best possible lineup each week (Max PF, using everyone on the roster including the bench). /wk is the average points per week dropped (left on the bench). The bar has 10 segments, one per league rank: #1 (most efficient) fills all 10.
Could have Won: matches you could have won if you played your optimal lineup. Lost: matches you could have lost if your opponent played their optimal lineup.
Median scoring adds a win each week for a top-5 score and a loss otherwise. Ties broken by PF.

RO_SECTION
Glossary
Rosters current Sleeper rosters. By position groups them, with injured reserve and taxi squad listed separately; By points lists every player from most to fewest points so you can see where a team's strength sits. No QBs drops quarterbacks; Pts/g sorts by points per game played instead of season total, so a player who missed games isn't buried, and the tile switches to his rank by points per game (ranked against every NFL player at the position with 3+ games; players with fewer games are slotted into that list). The coloured tile is the player's rank among every NFL player at that position by season points in this league's scoring (RB5 = fifth-most RB points); tied players share a rank. Pts season total, /g per game played. Bye week in blue if it is this week, dimmed once it has passed. The small badge is the player's NFL team in its colours; a red cross marks a player in an IR slot. The number after the name is age in years (from date of birth), coloured green for under 27, amber for 27–30 and red for 31 and over; each position heading shows the group's average age in the same colours.
MU_SECTION
Glossary
Matchups one option per game this week. Lineups shows each team's Sleeper starters slot by slot (QB, RB, RB, WR, WR, TE, flex, K, DEF), then active bench players: a shaded box marks a final score (green = beat the projection, red = fell short), grey = Sleeper's projection in this league's scoring; vs = home game, @ = away. Projected = points scored plus projections for players still to play. Q / D / O = questionable, doubtful, out. The tile is the player's season position rank. The card puts both teams side by side: seed tile, record, last five results (green win, red loss), points per week, PF rank, all-play and luck (the better value in white), any earlier meeting, and each team's active players on an NFL bye this week. Below it, switch between the two teams' results so far (running record on the right; HI / LO = that team had the week's top or bottom score) and remaining schedule (opponent's seed tile, record and points per week, plus how many of that team's active players are on a bye that week). Bye counts leave out IR and taxi players.
Slate groups starters by kickoff window: slate points (projected) and the running total. On the next slate still to finish, Needs over proj shows how many points the trailing team's remaining starters must score above their projections to catch up, assuming the leader's remaining starters hit theirs. Finished players count their actual score, live players the higher of actual and projection.
All-play record if you played all 9 teams every week. It measures how well you scored, with your schedule taken out.
Luck actual wins minus expected wins (shown as xW). Expected wins = all-play win % × games played. Example: 44-10 all-play is 81.5%, × 6 games = 4.89 expected wins; with 6 actual wins, luck is +1.11.
PO_SECTION
Glossary
Playoffs the top 6 by record (then points for) make it; seeds 1 and 2 get byes. Week 15: 3 v 6 and 4 v 5. Week 16: 1 plays the 4/5 winner, 2 plays the 3/6 winner (a fixed bracket, Sleeper's default). Week 17 is the final. Later rounds use the projected winner of each game, marked proj.; tap any game to open it.
Projections each team's best legal lineup (QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, TE, flex, K, DEF) from its current roster, leaving out IR and taxi. Player projections are Sleeper's for that week, scored with league settings. Win chance compares the two projected totals with a spread scaled to their size, the same way as the in-season odds.
Custom pick team A, team B and a playoff week to see that game.
CMP_SECTION
Glossary
Compare pick team A and team B from the two menus, then a topic. Throughout, the better value is in white and team A is on the left. Overview: season stats and any meeting this season. Scoring: both teams' weekly scores on one chart, consistency (Swing = standard deviation of weekly scores; lower is steadier), and Schedule swap: each team's record if its weekly scores had faced the other team's opponents. Positions: points per start, each team's best-ranked player, and how many startable players (top 12 QB, TE, K, DEF; top 24 RB, WR). Players: players paired off from best to worst at each position, points down the middle; Pts / Pts/g switches season total and points per game; with Pts/g the rank tiles show points-per-game ranks (against NFL players at the position with 3+ games). Taxi squad is left out; a red cross marks IR; the number after each name is the player's age (shaded by band: under 27, 27–30, 31+). Depth: bench points per week (rostered players not started), roster totals and how many players average 10+ per game. Schedule: remaining opponents' average seed and PF, games against the top 3, and players lost to NFL byes, then week by week. Dynasty: average age of QB, RB, WR and TE (plain and weighted by points) and rookie picks owned for 2027–2029 (number = round; blue outline = acquired from another team). Trades: trades each team has made and any trades between the two, with points the players have scored since.
SX_SECTION
Glossary
Points by position average points per player started at each position (season total ÷ number of starts), so using the flex on an RB or WR doesn't inflate that position. Per week is the season total ÷ games played and Total is the season total; both include flex starts. League rank underneath: #1 gold, 2–3 green, 8–9 red, #10 red with an outline, T = tied.
Odds each remaining game (Weeks 7–14) is simulated 20,000 times. A team's weekly score is drawn from its season average blended 65/35 with the league average, with a spread from its own and the league's week-to-week swing. Seeds go by wins, then points for. The 10 small bars under each name are its chance of finishing in seeds 1 to 10 (brighter = more likely; blue = playoff seeds). Proj W is average final wins.
Schedules each row is a team's real weekly scores played against the column team's schedule (if that schedule meets the row team, it faces the column team's score instead). The white square is the actual record; green = more wins with that schedule, red = fewer. Columns: RR Team Ram Rod, BP Bill's Block Party, HH Homer's Heroes, MA Mandingoes, RI Team RIPMFL, CT Cheating is Trying, CV Convicts, NF Nuts in the Face, AP Reverse Apache Master, CW Canton waitlist. Luck = actual wins minus the average across all 10 schedules.
Trades total points scored by the players each side received, from the first Sunday after the trade through the latest completed week, whether or not they were started. Picks and FAAB are listed but not scored. Brighter side = more points so far.
Lineups the biggest single cases of a bench player outscoring the starter he could have replaced (same position, or RB/WR/TE through the flex). Cost the win = that swap alone would have flipped a loss. Winnable = losses where the best possible lineup from the roster would have beaten the opponent's actual score.
Projections starters' points minus Sleeper's projections. Beat = weeks the lineup beat its projection. Players need 3+ starts; K and DEF excluded.
Margins record in games decided by under 10 points (close) and by 30 or more (blowout); Avg = average margin of victory or defeat.
Stars share of the team's season points (scored by starters) that came from its single top scorer (white) and its next two (grey).
Age share of starters' season points by each player's current age: 25 and under, 26–29, 30+; DEF has no age.
Ranks where each team's score ranked among all 10 that week (1 = highest), green for top 3, red for bottom 3; sorted by average.
Byes number of core starters (top QB, 2 RB, 3 WR, TE, K and DEF by season points on the current roster) on bye in each remaining regular-season week.
